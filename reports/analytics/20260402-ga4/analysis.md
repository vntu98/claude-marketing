## Key Findings

1. **Conversion count inflation:** `session_start` is marked as a conversion event, making every session appear as a conversion. The CPC and direct channels showing 5,547 conversions each are almost certainly measuring session starts, not purchases. True purchase conversions = 13 (`in_app_purchase`) over 30 days. This is a reporting integrity issue that affects every decision made using the acquisition report.

2. **Define depth is anomalously high:** 91,330 `activity_word_define` events from only 824 users means a small cohort is doing extremely heavy dictionary lookups (~111 per user). This cohort may be a "power dictionary" segment that is meaningfully different from the video-watch learner segment. They are likely under-monetized.

3. **Save rate is critically low:** Only 134 users save words (1,186 saves) out of 824 who define. Saving words is the activation signal in the tracking plan. The define → save conversion rate is roughly 16% on a user basis. Improving the save CTA or reducing friction here has the highest leverage on activation quality.

4. **Notes and vocab tabs drive the most tab traffic but are invisible post-entry:** 4,085 notes views and 430 vocab views have no corresponding `_complete` event. The team cannot tell if these tabs are delivering value or dead ends.

5. **Speaking tab completion is ~2.5%:** This is the highest-volume interactive tab (678 starts, 209 users) with the worst completion rate. Either the experience is too long/hard or users hit a bug. This is a product quality signal.

6. **(not set) attribution row is anomalous:** 1,160 active users attributed to `(not set)` source with only 153 sessions and 0% engagement rate. This is likely a data quality or SDK configuration issue and should not be left unaddressed — it distorts active-user counts.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Remove ecommerce template conversion events immediately** — Remove `add_to_cart`, `add_to_wishlist`, `begin_checkout`, `session_start`, `view_item`, `view_item_list`, `view_search_results` from GA4 Admin > Conversions. This restores accurate conversion reporting. Do this before any channel performance review or paid campaign optimization decisions.

2. **Add `activity_video_tab_notes_complete` and `activity_video_tab_vocab_complete` events** — Notes and Vocab are the two highest-entry tabs with no completion signal. Instrument completions so the funnel is measurable. Priority: high. Owner: product/engineering.

3. **Investigate and fix the (not set) attribution row** — 1,160 users with no session source and 0% engagement rate indicates a misconfigured stream or SDK event batch arriving outside of session context. Route to engineering for SDK audit.

4. **Add `activity_word_save` as a formal conversion or key event** — The tracking plan already names this as a supporting conversion. Mark it in GA4 Admin to surface it in acquisition reports and channel quality scoring.

5. **Diagnose speaking tab drop-off** — Pull a session recording or add an intermediate event (e.g., `activity_video_tab_speaking_prompt_view`) to identify where users abandon in the speaking flow. 2.5% completion on the most-started interactive tab is a retention risk.

6. **Verify `purchase` vs. `in_app_purchase` event overlap** — Both are registered as conversions from 2025-12-27. If they fire on the same transaction, revenue and conversion counts are double-counted. Confirm SDK behavior with engineering.

7. **Add Google Play search-term UTM enrichment or link from Play Console** — Google Play organic drives 11% of sessions with no campaign label. Link the GA4 property to Google Play Console to get store search terms and referral paths.

## Unresolved Questions

1. What is the date range for the snapshot? The JSON confirms `30daysAgo` but the actual calendar dates are not in the files — exact period matters for comparing to any historical baseline.
2. Is the `purchase` event a web-only event or a duplicate of `in_app_purchase` on mobile? The overlap cannot be confirmed from the snapshot.
3. Does the app have a web signup surface? If so, are web signups tracked in a separate GA4 property or the same one? The absence of `signup_completed` in the event list may indicate separate properties.
4. What triggered the batch of 7 ecommerce events added on 2026-03-20? Was this from a GTM template applied to the property? Understanding the source prevents recurrence.
5. Is `activity_video_tab_switch` (4,123 events) the same as physical tab navigation between the practice tabs, or something else? If it is a tab switch event, it may be double-counting tab entries already captured by `_start` and `_view` events.
